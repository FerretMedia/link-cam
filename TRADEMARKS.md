## Forking

A fork has to have its own name and icon. Replace the contents of `/brand`
(the app name in `brand/android/res/values/strings.xml`, the glyph and the
launcher icon) and change the application id from `cool.linker.cam`.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on this repository.
